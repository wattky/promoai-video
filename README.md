# Promo AI — Social Media Promo Video Generator

Create commercial 15-second and 20-second social media promo videos with an AI brief assistant and transparent, upfront pricing.

🌐 **Official Web App:** [https://promoai.video](https://promoai.video)

## Key Features
- **AI-Guided Brief Creation:** Plan your product or service promo video through a simple conversation before spending money.
- **Upfront Pay-Per-Video Pricing:** Review the exact price before confirming payment — no recurring monthly subscriptions.
- **Social-Ready Formats:** Export in **16:9** (Landscape), **9:16** (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts), and **1:1** (Square) at 1080p delivery resolution.
- **Commercial Rights:** Full commercial usage rights included (subject to input rights and Terms of Service).
- **Multilingual Workspace:** Available in English, Slovak, Czech, Polish, German, Hungarian, French, and Spanish.

## How It Works
1. Open [promoai.video](https://promoai.video) and describe your product or service.
2. Refine the brief, duration (15s or 20s), and aspect ratio with the Promo AI assistant.
3. Review the exact price and confirm payment to generate your video.
